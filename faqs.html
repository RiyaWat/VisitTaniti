Frequently Asked Questions

The legal drinking age on Taniti is 18. Note that alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.
You can use public buses in Taniti City (5 a.m.–11 p.m.), private island buses, taxis, bike rentals (helmet required), or explore walkable Taniti City and Merriton Landing on foot.
The U.S. dollar is widely accepted across hotels, shops, and restaurants on Taniti.
Yes. Taniti is a welcoming destination with a low crime rate. Taniti City and Merriton Landing are safe and easy to explore on foot.
