Transportation on Taniti

Getting around the island

Most visitors arrive by air, then choose from several easy ways to explore Taniti.

Rental Cars

Rent from a local agency near the airport to explore the island at your own pace.

Public Buses

Serve Taniti City daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. — affordable and easy for visitors.

Private Island Buses

Reach the rest of the island beyond Taniti City on private bus routes.

Taxis

Readily available throughout Taniti City for quick, direct trips.

Bike Rentals

Rent bikes from several vendors. Helmets are required by law and provided.

No car? No problem.

You can get around Taniti without renting a car using public buses (Taniti City, 5 a.m.–11 p.m.), private island buses, taxis, bike rentals (helmet required), or simply walking — Taniti City and Merriton Landing are very walkable.
